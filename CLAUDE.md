## Working rules (Lorcan's)
- Before any task, show a checklist of steps with a predicted timeline, and tick steps off as you go.
- **Accuracy first** (as close to 100% as possible; he accepts about 1 wrong in 100 for a much higher answer rate), then speed. End goal: every chocolate recognised correctly in under ~3 s. The scanner decides by itself: no tap-to-confirm; keep the short hold over the chocolate.
- **Fair testing:** every change is measured on held-back sessions it has never seen (`training/sessions.json`). Report % right when answering, % answered and the confusion list; never keep a change that makes the held-back result worse; rules chosen by looking at a session must be confirmed on the next one. Use `python3 training/scorecard.py compare` for any before/after claim and quote its verdict word for word (a proposed stronger wording is in Open decisions).
- Every test session is saved (`tests/test-N/` and in `~/Chocolate Scanner`); the model should learn from the mistakes in them.
- Client page look stays luxury and matches zchocolat.com (Cormorant Garamond + Jost, black and white, square edges, official logo and photos). New testing tools go on the admin page only. Everything runs on the phone. No paid services.
- After changing app files, bump `VERSION` in `app/sw.js` (`MODEL_VERSION` only if a model or gallery file changed; after any gallery change run `node tools/pack_gallery.js` first). After editing `app/index.html`, run `python3 tools/build-admin.py`.
- Run `tools/check.sh` (`--browser` for the browser test, `--photo` for the single-photo path) before Lorcan pushes. Work on `main` only, no feature branches (even if a task brief asks for one): commit each stage separately with a clear message so any one can be reverted on its own. Scratch scripts and outputs go in `experiments/` (gitignored).
- English first; French later if needed.

## How it works today (23 Nov 2026)
- Pipeline, all on the phone (`app/model6`): finder → quality gates (blur, size, dark, glare) → reader (chocolate, shell colour, mark type) × logic → straightening (edge maths + which-way-up model) → chocoprint (ArcFace embedding of the number, compared with a gallery of 1,409 real references) → decision.
- **The live rule (E + stop):** answer when reader and chocoprint agree (or the combined score is ≥ 99%), the chocoprint named it in ≥ 80% of the held frames and the shell colour fits (skipped for N°20); if not certain after **5 s of usable frames** (17 Nov, Lorcan's rule: a blurry or dark stretch pauses the clock; a gap between usable frames counts at most 0.4 s), or after 10 s on the wall clock whatever the frames did, no answer: "We could not be certain" and the likeliest three as chips. Both clocks are checked on every frame, rejected ones included. The 3–13 Nov forced guess is gone (it made 15 wrong instead of 7); the 13–17 Nov wall-clock 5 s answered one scan fewer (test-4 N°20) and was only checked on usable frames, so test-13 N°23 ran 12 s.
- **Finder look-again** (12 Nov): after its first pass the finder looks again at a window twice its own box, repeated until the box stops shrinking. Distance frames 3/17 → 9/17, close-ups unchanged.
- **Rotation re-check** (13 Nov; on the phone since test-13): a remembered which-way-up is only trusted while the chocoprint keeps matching at ≥ 0.9; after a weaker match the model runs again and the chocoprint judges the remembered rotation against the fresh one and its opposite. **Test-14 showed it thrashing on the fresh chocolates:** they match at a median 0.89, so the check runs on most frames, and an upside-down N°19 matches 14/21 at 0.86-0.9, so the rotation flipped 0°/180° up to 12 times in a scan; flips in 22 of 32 not-certain scans, 5 of 8 wrong, 36 of 84 right. A lead for a phone A/B after the exam (not replayable: the stored vector is the chosen rotation's only).
- **Settle gate** (13 Nov, not yet tried on a phone): the hold and the 5 s clock start only once the view has stayed roughly still over what the finder found (three frames within 0.15 of each other over ≥ 150 ms, or found for a second with no jump); until then the hint says "Hold still over the chocolate". Why: the finder says "found" on almost anything, so a camera switched on facing the ceiling used to start the clock on the room. On the saved sessions the gate opens a median 0.3 s after the first usable frame, never after the phone's own answer. The browser smoke test now drives the live loop on a static picture through this gate to an answer. Keep the 5 s timer: without it 12 more scans get answered of which 4 are wrong, and right answers take a median 0.8 s, 90% within 2.4 s.
- **6/9 pair check** (23 Nov, scanner-v65, live camera only, after three frames): when the chocoprint says 6 or 9 and the partner matches nearly as well turned over (within 0.1), the shell tally over the scan chooses which way up (6 dark, 9 milk). Why: the which-way-up model turns the fresh N°9 over and a turned 9 matches the gallery's 6 at 0.96-0.98. Replayed on all six held-back sessions: right 191 -> 196, wrong 14 -> 13, "can't tell from this many scans"; on single photos it made things worse, hence live only. Frames it turned are logged `pf: 1`.
- **Finder gate for Nothing shots** (23 Nov, admin page only until exam list 2 confirms it): a frame with finder confidence under 0.75 (`MIN_OBJ`) is rejected like a blurry one, so a blank card or a pen never starts the clock; no chocolate answer changes in the replay.
- **Reinforcement:** a top-confidence answer's chocoprint joins the gallery for the rest of the visit (clients only, off in admin test sessions, never fair-tested — Open decisions). A correction is "Choose the number instead" then a tray tap; a plain tray tap never touches the gallery.
- **Single-photo path** (camera unavailable, e.g. some in-app browsers): one frame, agree-or-99%, otherwise chips. Measured 13 Nov: within noise of the live rule.
- **First visit:** the camera opens while the models download (~8.4 MB: models and packed gallery 6.8 MB, TensorFlow 1.5 MB); the viewfinder shows the progress. Return visits are ready in 4–6 s.
- **Admin page extras:** the build (VERSION, MODEL_VERSION, model folder) shown above the test log and recorded in every entry, test log with Exam mode (23 Nov: three lists of 120, every chocolate 4 times then 8 Nothing shots with a subject to point at, each list keeping its place; since v64 the card says: right, wrong or not certain, tap Next and never scan the same one again) and a "Nothing" label, the chocoprint gap (top mark minus runner-up) in every trace frame (16 Nov), tilt permission asked inside the Scan tap (client page not yet), the finder's box drawn on the viewfinder (solid yellow = the box the reader gets, with its size, number of extra looks and the finder's confidence; dashed blue = the first look before the look-again moved it), torch, speed and camera tests, reference and manual-label capture rounds (Manual label saves photos at the camera square's own size, capped at 1600 px, since 16 Nov). The client page shows only the quiet white ring.
- **Branches:** none, by Lorcan's decision on 13 Nov. Everything is on `main`, which GitHub Pages publishes; the four branches made that week were merged into it the same day after they let the old site get phone-tested by mistake (test-12). Each stage is its own commit so a bad one can be reverted alone. Never switch branches in the shared working copy.

## Test 12 (13 Nov 2026, afternoon) - diagnosed, not a scanner regression
- **What it measured:** 28 scans, one per chocolate, on bare dark striped wood in low direct sun with hard shadows, the camera at an angle. It ran on the OLD live site (scanner-v51 with the 3-13 Nov forced guess: 18 of 28 answers forced, no "unsure" type, no look-again or tilt fields) because the new work was still on unmerged branches. Filed as a separate-condition session like test-8 to 11, not held back. Scan 27's label corrected 22 → 23 (the photo shows N°23). As it ran: 11 right. Forced guesses 4 of 18 right; rule-E answers 7 of 10 right (5→9, 7→4, 10→0). Under today's live rule (no answer past 5 s): 10 answered, 3 wrong, 18 "not certain" - dark chocolates 2 answered of 14 (both wrong), milk-and-white 8 of 14 (1 wrong). The reader called 827 of the dark chocolates' 870 frames "milk" and only 1 of 14 dark chocolates came out right.
- **Stage 3, the four versions of every close-up through the real reader and chocoprint** (A as saved; B re-cropped to the hand-measured number face at readerPad 1.35; C colour-balanced so the wood is neutral grey; D both):

  | | A as saved | B re-cropped | C colour-balanced | D both |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | dark chocolates read "dark" (of 14) | 0 | 6 | 0 | 6 |
  | chocoprint names the truth (of 28) | 13 | 9 | 14 | 10 |
  | reader names the truth (of 28) | 8 | 11 | 9 | 10 |
  | single-frame answers: answered / right / wrong | 8 / 6 / 2 | 7 / 6 / 1 | 8 / 6 / 2 | 8 / 6 / 2 |

- **Cause ranking:** (1) the loose finder box - the chocolate filled only about half the crop, with the sun's shadow inside the box (box median 0.41 of the view against a number face of 0.20); tightening it alone brings 6 of 14 dark shells back. (2) The sunlight itself on the chocolate: 8 of 14 dark shells still read "milk" with a tight box, and no colour balance of the background changes a single shell reading. (3) The forced guess, which turned every undecided scan into a wrong answer; it is already gone. The camera angle (a side face visible in most close-ups) rides along with (2). Limits: the saved close-ups are 224 px and the camera frames 320 px, so version B is an upscale of 1.5-4x; its chocoprint column is resolution-limited and says nothing about tight boxes, while the A-against-C comparison is fair (same pixels) and shows colour barely matters to the chocoprint.
- **Stage 4, the finder on the 28 frames against the measured boxes:** single pass hits at 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4 overlap 13 / 6 / 1 of 28, mean overlap 0.22; with look-again 24 / 14 / 7, mean 0.34, centre inside the face 22 of 28. Look-again tightened all 28 boxes and loosened none, but used all four extra looks every time and still ended at about 1.5 times the face - the shadow keeps it loose.
- **Recommended next step (not built):** a short reference round in direct sun and on a dark surface for the dark chocolates - 14 chocolates, 8 lamp-free sunlit shots each, on the dark table and the white card - added to the gallery, plus the same shots as shell-colour training photos off the white card for the reader; about 30 minutes of capture, an hour of ingest and fair-testing, and a reader retrain (~95 min of laptop time) only if the gallery alone does not move the dark-shell reading. Also a capture rule for the exam brief: no direct sun across the chocolate.

## Research review (13-16 Nov 2026) - verdict on Lorcan's write-up "How Recognition Systems Are Built"
- Checked claim by claim against the repo and the papers (full record in `docs/HISTORY.md`, "Research review"). The research is reported right bar one figure (the ArcFace "6 points for wrong labels" is a dataset swap, and our own mislabel fix measured as zero change). Its one new idea, flatten the number face from its four corners before reading, deserves a cheap oracle test but can fix at most 1-2 of the 7 held-back wrong answers (3 are rotation locks the re-check targets; 2 are side-on with no face to flatten), a tight box has not yet been shown to help the chocoprint (test-12 version B: 13 → 9), and a chocolate is a truncated pyramid with a raised number, which a warp cannot un-shade.
- **The oracle test, if run:** 60 close-up photos between 20° and 50° off overhead with the face at least a third of the frame, on the new chocolates; the chocoprint's margin to the true mark as the primary measure, the flip count second; scale-matched crops beside readerPad; no lookalikes as subjects; photos kept out of the finder's training. About a day of laptop work; nothing in `app/` until it has run.
- **What it exposed about us:** chocolate age and bloom are recorded nowhere and confound test-12's sun diagnosis (the only session on the old bloomed pieces); the phone's chocoprint and the laptop's have never been compared (same model, different pictures - `training/phone_vs_laptop.js`); "not a chocolate" needs a negative class, not a threshold; two corrected truths sat under their old photo folders (moved 16 Nov); the 12 Nov finder photos had gone with the deleted worktrees (re-filed).

## Known limits
- **Camera tilt / angle:** only roughly overhead shots work. Two code-only corrections and two synthetic oblique retrains were tried and rejected; steep angles are a real ceiling. The gyroscope hint nudges people flatter but needs the tilt permission (Open decisions 1).
- **Distance:** the finder is excellent close up and lost at arm's length; look-again fixes the middle range. The app refuses chocolates under 13% of the view (`MIN_SIZE`); the models cope down to about 9%, but the sharpness check (`MIN_SHARP` 45) would refuse most such frames, so both must move together, calibrated from a real session's logged size and sharpness.
- **Lookalikes:** 20↔21/23/24, 13 with 15/18/19/21/23 (a design-level ambiguity in the artwork), 6/9, 12/22, 4/7, 5/6, 22/16; N°16 is worth watching. Two tie-breakers against the official designs failed because the same bad crop poisons both galleries. **Test-14: the fresh N°19 reads as 13** (6 of 9 scans; the chocoprint says 13 at 0.8-0.93) and N°9 as 6 when the which-way-up puts it at 180° (twice now, test-13 and test-14): 6/9 is a which-way-up failure (fixed live by the 6/9 pair check, v65, to confirm); 13/19 is batch drift - the fresh N°19 is as close to the old gallery's 13 as to its 19, while fresh 19s match each other well - so it needs a reference round of the fresh pieces (docs/HISTORY.md, 23 Nov evening).
- **Not a chocolate:** nothing in the pipeline can say "this is not a zChocolat chocolate"; an empty desk is "found" at 99% with a chocoprint match as strong as a real answer. Test-14 (23 Nov) has the first 10 Nothing shots on disk (box lid and side, the white card, a hand, the bare table, the QR card, a pen): 5 answered "zwhite" (pale things with no mark), the box side ran to 90% for N°24. The finder's confidence separates the answered ones from every chocolate scan (0.56-0.74 against 1.00): the `minobj` lead, to confirm on sittings 2-3 by replay.
- **Tilt logging:** no saved session has a tilt value, because the permission request ran outside the Scan tap and iOS refused it silently; fixed on the admin page only, and the outcome is now recorded in every test-log entry.
- **Glare gate** (`MAX_GLARE` 0.03): never triggered in eight sessions; unvalidated. The three Z chocolates are hard to tell apart by mark alone; the reader's shell colour corrects most of it.
- **Reinforcement** has never been measured in a live session (a single-frame replay of test-4..7 showed 25 reinforcements, all on right answers, and no outcome change).
- **Chocolate age and bloom:** the pieces photographed since 20 Oct were 3-4 weeks old and bloomed by 13 Nov; no session or reference round records a batch date, so test-12's pale dark shells may be bloom as much as sun. Clients receive chocolates after at least two days of travel, so the gallery and the exam should cover more than fresh pieces (Open decisions 5).

## Tools
- `tools/check.sh [--browser] [--photo] [--update-baseline]` — the pre-push check: syntax, self-tests, packed-gallery sync, the model-6 quality baseline, the live-rule baseline (`training/baseline-ruleE.txt`), the browser smoke test, the photo path (`training/baseline-photo.txt`). Run as `bash tools/check.sh` if the executable bit is missing.
- `python3 training/scorecard.py` — every held-back number with its range and the Nothing table; `compare <before> <after>` — the verdict (replay rules such as `E`, `LIVE`, `LIVE+obj75` the finder-confidence gate, `E+forced5`, a JSON rule, or two DUMP files; two rule names also replay the Nothing shots under each); `risk` — which logged signals predict a wrong answer (the chocoprint gap joins it on sessions from scanner-v59); `selftest`.
- `python3 training/exam_report.py tests/test-14 [tests/test-15 ...]` — an exam sitting scored as planned (each list position once, by its first attempt) and over every scan, with the retakes, the Nothing shots, the confusions, per-chocolate results and the time to a right answer; pooled when several sittings are given.
- `node training/phone_vs_laptop.js tests/test-N` — the phone's chocoprint on each scan against the laptop's from the saved close-up (training/serving skew); crude mode on sessions without the logged vector.
- `python3 training/trace_replay.py tests/test-N [new|sweep]` — the live rule and variants replayed from a session's traces; its first "new" row is what step 4 checks.
